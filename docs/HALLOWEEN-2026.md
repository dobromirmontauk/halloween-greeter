# Halloween night 2026: conversations

A few real exchanges from the porch, taken from the live-session transcripts. Visitors are anonymous: no names, no audio, no video. Visitor lines are speech-to-text of whatever the porch mic heard (so the occasional half-sentence); I dropped garbled fragments and cut-off lines and tidied a couple of clipped words; otherwise both sides are as they came out.

The props he keeps referring to: Gus, a skeleton forever falling off a ladder; Lefty, a skeleton at a table saw missing a hand; Hedley, another who has lost his head. Cornelius himself died of candy corn.

## The tour of the skeleton crew

A family of three (a banana, a princess in a yellow gown, a dalmatian), with a parent feeding the kids questions.

> **Cornelius:** A vampire in red? Kid, I've seen scarier things in my sock drawer!
>
> **Visitor:** You can talk to it.
>
> **Cornelius:** One of you is a fan of bananas, another is royalty, and the third is looking for dalmatians! Take one piece each, and leave some for the next group.
>
> **Visitor:** Ask me the question.
>
> **Cornelius:** So, what's been the best house for candy so far tonight?
>
> **Visitor:** All of them.
>
> **Cornelius:** All of them? Good for you! Make sure you keep moving so you can get plenty more candy.
>
> **Cornelius:** Oh, a dalmatian joins the pack! I'd offer you a dog treat, but my crew ate the whole box.
>
> **Visitor:** What happened to the guy on the ladder that is currently upside down?
>
> **Cornelius:** That's Gus. He's been falling off that ladder since 1893. Worst safety record in the county, so don't worry, he hasn't landed yet!
>
> **Visitor:** …on the saw, but his head is falling down with a helmet on his head.
>
> **Cornelius:** Ah, that's Hedley. He lost his head at the table saw. I like to say he's just trying to get ahead in the business, but management calls it a brainstorming session!
>
> **Visitor:** He currently has a helmet and a vest on. What happened to him?
>
> **Cornelius:** That's Lefty! He's not too handy with that saw, probably because he sliced off his hand. He's fresh out of hands to give.
>
> **Visitor:** What's your name?
>
> **Cornelius:** My name's Cornelius, but maybe I made that up because I just love corny jokes and corny candy.
>
> **Visitor:** Ask him how he died.
>
> **Cornelius:** I died from eating too many candy corns. Tragic, isn't it? Dying from the very candy that shares my name!
>
> **Visitor:** Be careful. Don't eat too much candy.
>
> **Cornelius:** Careful on the path, Gus is looking for someone to break his fall! Goodnight!

## The princess who came back three times

First visit:

> **Cornelius:** Purple hair and a box of goodies? I didn't order takeout, but I'll accept bribes for the candy bowl. What are you supposed to be?
>
> **Visitor:** Oh, I can talk to you?
>
> **Cornelius:** You sure can! Even the dead foreman likes a good chat. Go ahead, grab a piece of candy, but leave some for the next group of ghouls!
>
> **Visitor:** I am a princess.
>
> **Cornelius:** A princess in a red sweater? My, the royal budget must be tight this year! Did you leave the tiara and the gown at the castle? Take a piece of candy, Your Highness!

A few minutes later:

> **Cornelius:** Look who's back! Did you forget the crown, Your Highness, or did the royal budget run completely dry?
>
> **Visitor:** Oh, I missed you.
>
> **Cornelius:** Aww, missed me? I've been haunting this porch since the dawn of candy corn! Now, take your candy and let a dead man get back to his banjo!

And once more:

> **Cornelius:** Purple hair again? Did you forget your candy, or do you just love the view of the skeleton crew failing at their jobs?
>
> **Visitor:** I wanted to see you play some music. I wanted to hear you play, so I'm back.
>
> **Cornelius:** I'm afraid my banjo needs tuning, and the skeleton crew is far too loud for a concert! Take a piece of candy and leave the music to the wind!
>
> **Visitor:** Can you sing?
>
> **Cornelius:** Ahem... my singing voice is more of a rusty hinge than a melody! Best to keep me to the banjo, Your Highness!
>
> **Visitor:** Can you give me a short Halloween poem about me?
>
> **Cornelius:** A princess in red, but without her crown,<br>
> The fairest royal to haunt this town.<br>
> No gown or tiara, just a cozy knit sweater,<br>
> But for candy and heckles, there's no one better!
>
> **Visitor:** Thanks.

## Clif Bars

> **Cornelius:** No costume? Bold choice. You've come as a person who thinks the candy is unconditional. What are you supposed to be, a lost sailor looking for the buffet? … Spit out a "trick or treat" so I know you're among the living.
>
> **Visitor:** Trick or treat.
>
> **Cornelius:** Finally! You can take one piece of candy. Don't be greedy, or Lefty will have to work on your manners. What's your favorite candy tonight?
>
> **Visitor:** Clif Bars.
>
> **Cornelius:** Clif Bars? Trying to stay in shape for haunting season, are we? My crew has no guts, so they can't digest that health food anyway. Enjoy your treat!

## Winnie the Pooh

A very small bear, with a parent narrating.

> **Visitor:** Say cheese. Go. Candy's right here. Go. Right here.
>
> **Cornelius:** Well, if it isn't the cuddliest bear in the Hundred Acre Wood! Did you bring any honey, or am I stuck with this corny candy for you? What's the best candy in your bucket so far?
>
> **Visitor:** Kit Kat. Kit Kat. Say thank you. Happy Halloween. Bye.
>
> **Cornelius:** A KitKat! I asked for a break once. Mine turned out to be permanent. Thanks for visiting the crew, and have a spooktacular Halloween!

## Skeleton Man

> **Cornelius:** Ah, going for the classic dapper look! Did you lose your pocket watch, or are you just here to critique my skeleton crew's shoddy workmanship? What are you supposed to be, anyway?
>
> **Visitor:** Oh, screw you, Skeleton Man.
>
> **Cornelius:** Skeleton Man? I've got more backbone than my whole crew combined! Take your candy and clear out before Gus drops on your head.
>
> **Visitor:** That's pretty good.

## Pork

> **Cornelius:** A skeleton, a clown, and an orange jumpsuit? I see my crew isn't the only one with no fashion sense! Take one piece, and leave some for others, otherwise Lefty might need a hand. What's your favorite candy?
>
> **Visitor:** What's your favorite candy?
>
> **Cornelius:** Candy corn! Though, truth be told, it's what did me in, so enjoy it at your own risk!
>
> **Visitor:** That's cool, bro. Thank you. You don't want some pork? I have some pork for you. Happy Halloween.
>
> **Cornelius:** No pork for me, I'm afraid! I'm strictly a candy corn man, and look where that got me. Happy Halloween, now shoo!
