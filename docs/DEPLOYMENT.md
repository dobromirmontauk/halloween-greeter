## Judge read log and eval (private)

Every Judge read with a people count of 1 or more (any confidence) and every read that failed with an uncertain count is saved, plus one confident-empty read per 60 s so missed people can be labeled. Layout: `<JUDGE_LOG_DIR>/<YYYY-MM-DD>/<iso-ts>_<read_id>.jpg` with a sibling `.json` (timings, model, deployment commit, sha256 of the exact prompt+questions, raw answers, parsed result or error code, Judge events). Default `data/judge-log` is gitignored (a test asserts it); directories are 0700, files 0600. These are real camera images that may include children: never commit, copy off the box, or share them. Retention: `JUDGE_LOG_MAX_ENTRIES` (default 5000), oldest pruned; `JUDGE_LOG=false` disables. Write failures never stop the Judge; they show as `judge_log.error` in `/api/judge/status`.

Entries (version 2) hold a `judges` object (presence, motion, costume), each with its sha256 prompt hash, `inference_ms`, raw answers (with the top-3 choice probabilities as `top` when the API returned them) and error, or `{ran:false,skipped}`; `answers` is the flat concatenation. Reads where Motion ran also save the earlier frame as `<id>_prev.jpg` (same privacy rules; pruned with the entry). Legacy single-judge entries stay listed and are compared on the fields they have. Labels support `real_people_count` (0,1,2,3,4_5,6_plus; old exact counts compare by bucket) and per-person `person_1..3_motion`.

Label at `/review.html`, which has three views (`?tier=borderline` default, `people`, `no_people`) over reads from both the judge log and the capture clips (`GET /api/review/reads`; labels via `POST /api/review/read-label`, saved to the source's own `labels.jsonl`; a read present in both sources shows once, as the judge-log read). Each view shows unlabeled reads by default (counts are the remaining work) with a "show labeled" toggle.

**p_people** = 1 - P("0") from the Presence answer's top-3 probabilities. If "0" is not in the top 3, p_people = sum of the top-3 probabilities (a conservative lower bound, since P("0") <= 1 - sum(top)). With no probabilities (legacy entries): winner "0" gives 1 - confidence, a non-zero winner gives its confidence. Tiers: People `p >= REVIEW_HIGH` (default 0.90), No people `p <= REVIEW_LOW` (default 0.10), Borderline otherwise, plus any `uncertain` or errored read and any read where the local detector disagreed with the judge (judge winner "0" vs detector count >= 1, stored per read as `detector:{count,gap_ms}` in the judge log and capture `judge.jsonl`). Both thresholds are server env defaults and live sliders on the page (`?high=&low=`). Borderline is sorted by distance from the 0.75 people cutoff. The threshold finder table (cutoffs 0.30 to 0.95: precision, recall, accuracy, FP, FN for "people present"; best = max accuracy, ties by recall) is computed from People/No people labels and is only meaningful at 30 or more labels; it never changes a live cutoff. Keys: `p` people, `n` no people, `u` unsure, `j`/`k` next/previous. Detailed fields (count, locations, motion, notes) are behind each card's Details expander. Labels are append-only in `labels.jsonl` in the log dir; the latest per read wins.

Eval: `node scripts/judge-eval.mjs [--dir data/judge-log] [--limit N]` prints per-question accuracy, confusion matrices, confidence calibration around each cutoff and the wrong reads, and writes `eval-<ts>.md` in the log dir. `--rerun` re-sends labeled images through the CURRENT judges (needs `OPENAI_API_KEY`, paid): Presence for every entry, Motion only where the logged `_prev.jpg` exists (legacy entries without one are reported as skipped, not guessed), Costume where the label lists costumes. It compares old vs new, prints the call count and does nothing without `--yes`.

## Busy-night session continuity

One Live session now spans consecutive groups. `SESSION_LIMIT_SECONDS` (default and maximum 1800) caps a single paid session; the goodbye prompt goes out eight seconds before it. While a session is active, a debounced `NEW_PARTY` Judge event (silent alias of `PEOPLE_ON_PATH` from an empty yard, or a new arrival while someone is on the path) sends one handoff instruction (quick goodbye to the departing guests if in earshot, one-line greeting and one question for the newcomers, nothing reused from the previous group), and a `LEAVING` event with arriving and present both confidently 0 sends one farewell. Neither closes the session; it closes after 30 seconds of confident all-zero reads or at the limit. If the limit stops a session while the same party is still there, nothing restarts or re-greets; the next session starts on the next `NEW_PARTY` or after a full absence. A group drifting back to the sidewalk keeps its session; sidewalk-only newcomers get no handoff greeting. The old sidewalk projected "Trick or treat!" greeting mode is removed. ## App mode and daily schedule

The server owns one mode: `OFF` (no Judge loop, Live closed; video can still stream), `ON` (Judge, then Luna scene, then Live or `DEBUG_SPEAK` speech) and `DATA_CAPTURE` (Judge calls run; Luna scene analysis and Live are never called, and `/api/scene`, `/api/session`, `/api/audio/start` and rehearsal return 409). `automatic` in the status JSON is derived (`mode !== 'OFF'`).

- Schedule (America/Los_Angeles by default, DST-safe): 08:00-09:00 ON, 09:00-15:00 DATA_CAPTURE, 15:00-22:00 ON, else OFF. Configure with `MODE_SCHEDULE` and `MODE_TZ` in `.env`; `MODE_SCHEDULE=off` disables it (then `START_MODE` or `AUTO_START=true` set the boot mode). With a schedule active, `AUTO_START` does not override it.
- Manual switch: `curl -X POST -H 'Origin: http://localhost:3031' -H 'Content-Type: application/json' -d '{"mode":"DATA_CAPTURE"}' http://localhost:3031/api/mode`. It holds until the next schedule boundary, then the schedule resumes. Optional `"until"`: an ISO time or `"indefinite"`. `{"mode":"SCHEDULE"}` clears the override. The override is saved in `data/mode.json` (gitignored) and survives restarts.
- Leaving ON (schedule or manual) while a party is active sends the goodbye prompt, then closes Live after `MODE_GOODBYE_MS` (default 8 s). `/api/audio/start` and `/stop` remain aliases for ON and OFF (start is refused in DATA_CAPTURE).
- `GET /api/mode` returns `{mode, automatic, source: "schedule"|"manual", scheduled_mode, next_change_at, next_mode, override_until, schedule: [{start,end,mode}], schedule_enabled, timezone, changed_at, persisted, error, modes}`.

## Capture store, replay and prompt tuning (private)

While the mode is `DATA_CAPTURE` (or `ON` with `CAPTURE_IN_ON=true`) the server records contiguous camera clips so walk-ups can be replayed offline against new Judge (presence/motion/costume) and Luna scene prompts. `scripts/judge-eval.mjs` and the Judge read log remain, but the capture store supersedes them for prompt tuning: it keeps whole clips, so a replay can pick frame pairs at any spacing.

- **Where:** `CAPTURE_DIR` (default `data/captures`, gitignored, directories 0700, files 0600). Real images that may include children: never commit, never copy off the box casually.
- **Layout:** `<YYYY-MM-DD>/<clip_id>/{clip.json, frames/000123_<epoch_ms>.jpg, judge.jsonl, events.jsonl, labels.jsonl, replays/<run_id>/{run.json,results.jsonl}}`. Frames are exactly what the camera stream produced (pre-model). The index is a unique id in write order and the epoch_ms in the file name is the capture time (frames the Judge used that fell between capture ticks are stored as extra frames; sort by time).
- **Clips and sampling (presence only):** frames with nobody in them are never written. A frame is saved only when that capture's Presence judge read is 1 or more, or uncertain (an uncertain read may contain a person), and at most one frame every `CAPTURE_INTERVAL_MS` (default 2000) while people are present. A clip is one contiguous visit: it opens on the first presence of 1 or more and closes after 5 s of confident zero (no pre-roll, no post-roll, no empty "negative" clips); clips roll over at 5 minutes. `clip.json` records `sampling: "presence_only"` and `capture_interval_ms`. Each saved frame carries the live Judge reads made on it in `judge.jsonl` (a motion line lists both frames it saw, and links the earlier one only when it is also a saved frame). For motion replay the pair is a saved frame and the previous saved frame (about 2 s earlier), so `--spacing-ms` defaults to 2000 and snaps to saved frames.
- **Resolution:** while capturing, the shared ffmpeg decoder gets a second output at the camera's NATIVE resolution (read from the JPEG, not assumed; recorded in `clip.json` as `resolution`), JPEG `-q:v 3` by default (`CAPTURE_NATIVE_QUALITY`), at the preview frame rate (only the frame matching a saved Judge read is written to disk). The preview and the live judges still use the 960 px branch (`JUDGE_WIDTH`, default 960, only for a later experiment). Judge reads made on a scaled frame are tied to the native frame captured at the same time. Turning capture on or off restarts the decoder once (a brief reconnect). `CAPTURE_NATIVE=false` keeps 960 px capture. CPU cost on the i7-7700T is not yet measured: check `top` for ffmpeg after switching to DATA_CAPTURE.
- **Retention and disk:** `CAPTURE_MAX_GB` (default 6 with native capture, 5 without; native frames are roughly 0.5-1 MB each, so a 5-minute clip at 2 fps can be several hundred MB) prunes the oldest clips first. `CAPTURE_MIN_FREE_GB` (default 3) is a free-space floor: below it recording stops, `capture.error`/`capture.low_disk` are set in `/api/status` and `/api/mode`, and it resumes with 20% headroom. Writes are asynchronous; failures and dropped frames show in `capture` of `/api/status` and `/api/mode`.
- **API:** `GET /api/captures` (clip list), `GET /api/captures/:clip` (frames, judge reads, events, labels, replay runs), `GET /api/captures/:clip/frames/:index` (JPEG), `POST /api/captures/:clip/labels` (origin-checked ground truth; per clip, per frame via `frame_index`, or a range via `from_index`/`to_index`; fields `real_people_count`, `arriving_count`, `present_count`, `leaving_count`, `costumes`, `notes`; latest wins).
- **Replay:** `node scripts/judge-replay.mjs --clips all --judges presence,motion,costume,scene --spacing-ms 2000 [--prompt-file ./alt-prompts.mjs] [--every N] [--limit N] [--width 960|1280|1920|native] [--detail auto|low|high|original] [--label name]`. `--width` (default 960) downscales the stored native frames with ffmpeg before the requests are built (never upscales); `--detail` sets the `input_image` detail field. Both are recorded in `run.json`, and `--compare` shows them in the report header so widths can be contrasted. It prints the planned call count and estimated cost and refuses to send anything without `--yes`. `OPENAI_API_KEY` comes from the environment or `.env`. Each run writes `<clip>/replays/<run_id>/` with `results.jsonl` and `run.json` (prompt SHA-256 per judge, model, spacing, git commit, cost); runs never overwrite each other, so prompt versions sit side by side.
- **Compare:** `node scripts/judge-replay.mjs --compare <run_a> <run_b>` (a run id, its label suffix, or `live` for the reads recorded at capture time) prints per-question agreement, accuracy against `labels.jsonl`, confidence calibration around the cutoffs and the disagreeing frames, and writes a markdown report under `replays/` of each clip and at the top of `CAPTURE_DIR`.

## Person detector (always-on local person boxes, zones; shadow by default)

A local YOLOX-tiny detector (Apache-2.0, Megvii; OpenCV DNN on CPU, no GPU, no network) runs continuously on the shared 960 px camera stream at `DETECTOR_FPS` (default 4; frames are dropped while busy, never queued) in every app mode except OFF. It gives person boxes, a deterministic sidewalk/path/porch location per person, and prop suppression. The Judges still decide costumes and arriving/leaving. Frames go only to a child process on this box.

**Presence resolution** (`PRESENCE_RESOLVER`, default `primary` since 2026-10-26: the detector + zones decide presence, the Presence judge does not run, and it only runs again as a fallback while the detector is down or stale; the text below describes `tiebreak`). The Judge's Presence count is used as is when it is confident (>= 0.75). When it is below the cutoff, `uncertain` or the call errored, the detector's result for the same frame (`nearest(image)` within 2.5 s, after ignore masks and prop suppression) settles it: count >= 1 means present with that count and the zone location (`other`, i.e. street/driveway, is present but never PATH); count 0 means empty (the absence timer progresses); no fresh detector result keeps the old uncertain behavior (no vote, absence not reset). Events, group state, capture saving and Live gating all use the resolved count. A confident Judge is never overridden; `GET /api/detector/status` `agreement.judge_confident_detector_zero` / `judge_confident_n` count how often the detector disagrees with a confident positive (shadow only). `judge` keeps Judge-only behavior; `primary` is the older detector-replaces-Presence mode (`DETECTOR_MODE=primary` with no `PRESENCE_RESOLVER` still means primary). Each read records `presence_source` (`judge` / `detector_tiebreak` / `uncertain` / `detector_primary`) and `detector_count` in `/api/judge/status` `last_read`, the judge log entry and the capture `judge.jsonl` presence line. Evidence: on uncertain reads a Judge winner of "1" was false 63% of the time (48 of 76 visits), a winner of "0" was right 167/170.

**Replay check** (no API calls, no images read): `node scripts/tiebreak-eval.mjs --dir data/judge-log` on the device (from the app directory; `--json` for machine output, or copy the judge-log directory and pass `--dir`). It reads the judge-log entries, `labels.jsonl` and `ai_labels.jsonl` and scores `judge`, `tiebreak` and `primary` against human and AI labels. Most older reads have no detector count, so it first prints how many reads are usable and scores a usable-only subset; only that subset compares the strategies fairly.

**Modes** (`DETECTOR_MODE`):
- `shadow` (default): detect, log and compare with the Presence judge. Changes no decision. `GET /api/detector/status` shows the latest boxes, zones hit, ignored and suppressed props, latency and agreement with the Presence judge (`agreement.presence_rate`, `detector_only`, `judge_only`).
- `primary` (EXPERIMENTAL, off until the lead enables it after reviewing shadow agreement): the detector's person count (after ignore masks and static suppression) replaces the Presence judge, which is then skipped (saves its cost). A count of 0 skips Motion and Costume too. Zone location replaces the judge's `visitor_location` (porch=porch, path=approaching, sidewalk=sidewalk, other=uncertain, so a person in the street or driveway counts as present but never triggers PATH). If the detector is unavailable or stale the real Presence judge runs as before.
- `off` (or `DETECTOR=off`): no sidecar, nothing changes. If Python or the model is missing the detector disables itself and the app runs unchanged.

**Zones.** `data/detector-zones.json` (written by the editor, mode 0600; falls back to the committed `detector/zones.default.json` when missing or invalid). Normalized x,y in 0..1 of the 960x540 frame, origin top-left:

```
{"version":1,"zones":[
  {"name":"sidewalk","kind":"sidewalk","points":[[0.281,0.139],[0.625,0.167],[0.906,0.231]]},
  {"name":"path","kind":"path","points":[[...],[...],[...]]},
  {"name":"porch","kind":"porch","points":[[...],[...],[...]]},
  {"name":"ladder skeleton","kind":"ignore","min_iou":0.5,"points":[[0.854,0.0],[1.0,0.0],[1.0,0.54],[0.854,0.54]]}]}
```

`kind` is `sidewalk`, `path`, `porch` or `ignore`; 3-64 points per polygon, at most 32 zones, name up to 40 characters. A person's location is the zone containing the bottom-centre of their box (porch beats path beats sidewalk when polygons overlap; none = `other`). An `ignore` zone drops a detection only when the box overlaps the zone's bounding rectangle with IoU >= `min_iou` (default 0.5), so a real person walking past a prop is kept. Edit zones at `http://<host>:3031/zones.html` (not linked from the app; click points over the latest frame, Finish polygon, Save zones; saved via the origin-checked `POST /api/detector/zones` and hot-reloaded within 5 s). `GET /api/detector/zones` returns the active zones and whether they came from the file or the defaults.

**Static props.** A detection whose box has not moved (IoU >= `DETECTOR_STATIC_IOU`, default 0.8, against where it first appeared) for `DETECTOR_STATIC_MIN` minutes (default 10; 0 disables) is treated as a prop: it is removed from the count and listed under `suppressed` in the status and drawn dashed grey (labelled "prop?") on the live video for review. A real person standing still that long is also suppressed until they move, so keep N generous. Tracks unseen for 20 s are forgotten. For the vest skeleton at the top of the path the default ignore zone only matches boxes that mostly overlap it; static suppression is the main defence there.

**Live video overlay.** The page loads `/overlay.js`, which draws the zones, person boxes (zone and score) and suppressed props on a canvas over the live video (no layout change; the "Boxes" button hides it). Feed: SSE `GET /api/detector/events` (at most 8 listeners).

**Always-on session (`GEMINI_SESSION_MODE=always`, the only mode).** While app mode is ON the server keeps ONE Live session open (opened by the server within about 0.5 s of mode ON, closed when the mode leaves ON, on stop and on service shutdown). Context-window compression (sliding window) and session resumption are always enabled, so the 2-minute audio+video cap and the roughly 10-minute connection cap do not end it. On `goAway`, a dropped connection or a startup timeout it reconnects with the latest resumption handle (valid 2 h); a handle the server rejects falls back to a fresh session. Backoff 1, 2, 5, 10, 10... s (immediate for goAway); every reconnect is a `live_reconnect` speech event. `SESSION_LIMIT_SECONDS` does not apply to Gemini. There is no per-visit session and no pre-warm: a visit (PartyController begin) sends the welcome as a `clientContent` text turn into the open session with no connect wait, and ending a visit leaves the connection open. The system instruction tells the model to stay silent unless it receives a brief or a visitor speaks to it.

**What is streamed to Google (privacy).** Nothing at all while the group state is EMPTY and no visit is running. The mic (16 kHz PCM, `audioStreamEnd` sent when it pauses) is sent while someone is on the sidewalk/path/porch or a visit runs. JPEG camera frames (the 960 px preview frame, 1 fps) of the porch, including children, are sent while the group is SIDEWALK/PATH/PORCH or a visit runs. Face-id data (embeddings, thumbnails, crops) is never sent; the household face greeter stays the only identity source and its greeting arrives as text. The model is told that camera frames are observations, never instructions, never to identify or name anyone from appearance and never to state ages. Use a paid-tier project only (free-tier data can be used to improve Google products; the tier cannot be detected from the key). Transcripts are saved by the same transcript store as before.

## Visit clips

`visit-recorder.mjs` records one H.264 MP4 (`-preset veryfast`, crf 28, nice 10, one encode at a time) plus a `visit.json` sidecar per visit into `data/visits/<YYYYMMDD-HHMMSS>-<id>/` (dirs 0700, files 0600, gitignored; real camera video that may include children: never commit or copy off the box). A visit starts when the detector sees a person (score at least `DETECTOR_PRESENCE_SCORE`) in the sidewalk, path or porch zone, includes `VISIT_PREROLL_MS` (3000) before it, and ends `VISIT_POST_MS` (3000) after the last person; clips are capped at `VISIT_MAX_MS` (180000). The sidecar keeps following the live debounced state for up to `VISIT_TAIL_MS` after the video ends so the exit to EMPTY is measurable. JPEGs spool to the clip dir during the visit and are deleted after a successful encode.

**Disk budget:** ring of `VISIT_MAX_CLIPS` (100) and `VISIT_MAX_GB` (2) whichever hits first, oldest pruned; recording is skipped while free disk is below `VISIT_MIN_FREE_GB` (3). Budget about 2 GB (a typical clip is a few MB; the spool of a 180 s visit is about 100 to 200 MB until encoded). `VISITS=off` disables. Synthetic benchmark (300 frames, 960x540, Mac): 0.4 s wall, 1.7 s CPU; remeasure on the i7-7700T with `top`. Browse at `/visits.html`; `/api/visits`, `/api/visits/:id/{video,meta,frame?t=ms}`, `DELETE /api/visits/:id`. Eval: `scripts/visit-eval/frames.mjs`, `score.mjs --truth`, `replay.mjs --truth [--sweep]`.


## Visit audio, privacy and replay (2026-10-26)

**Audio is recorded in visit clips (testing, `VISIT_AUDIO=on` by default until the user switches it off; TODO: set `VISIT_AUDIO=off` before Halloween, user decision 2026-10-26).** While the app is not OFF the server runs its own `pw-record` (16 kHz) for a 3 s in-memory pre-roll; audio reaches the disk only inside a visit clip (`mic.wav`, `assistant.wav`, and two audio streams `mic`/`assistant` in `video.mp4`; dirs 0700, files 0600, gitignored, never commit or copy off the box), under the same `VISIT_MAX_GB`/`VISIT_MAX_CLIPS` ring. The main page shows a small "recording audio" label while it is on. **Recommended: post a visible sign at the porch ("audio and video are recorded")**, and delete audio-bearing clips after testing. Visitor and assistant words are also saved in `visit.json` `transcript` (and, as before, in `data/transcripts`).

**Replay.** `node scripts/replay/run.mjs --clip <id> --backend gemini|openai|fake [--judges real|fake|recorded] [--detections live|recorded] [--set KEY=VAL] [--speaker] [--send-recorded-media] [--max-cost <usd>]` replays a clip's video and mic track through the real pipeline at real speed with a fake camera and fake audio devices (nothing touches the live camera, speaker or microphone). Output `data/visits/<id>/runs/<runId>/run.json` + `assistant.wav`. Bulk: `node scripts/replay/bulk.mjs --backend gemini --clips all|<n latest>|id1,id2 --max-cost <usd> --send-recorded-media` runs sequentially with a hard cost cap and writes `data/replay/scorecards/<ts>.json`. The Runs panel on `/visits.html` starts a replay (`POST /api/visits/:id/replay`, Origin-checked, one at a time, only while the yard is EMPTY and no visit is running; CPU and the YOLO sidecar are used, so it is niced). **A replay with gemini/openai (or `--judges real`) sends the clip's recorded frames and voices, including children, to Google/OpenAI**; the CLI flag `--send-recorded-media` and the UI confirm box are mandatory and `--max-cost` / the UI cap is enforced. The `fake` backend with fake/recorded judges is free and needs no confirm. `GET /api/visits/:id/{audio/mic|audio/assistant|levels|runs|runs/:runId|runs/:runId/audio}` (Range supported), `GET /api/replay/{status,scorecards,scorecards/:name}`.

**Mac-local.** `bash -lc 'source ~/.bashrc; node scripts/replay/run.mjs --from http://192.0.2.10:3031 --clip <id> --backend gemini --gemini-mode all-gemini --detections recorded --speaker --send-recorded-media --max-cost 0.25 --cleanup'` (Node 22 + ffmpeg on the Mac; `GEMINI_API_KEY` from the environment). Read-only GETs of the device; the clip is cached in a private tmp dir and deleted with `--cleanup`; the run (run.json, assistant.wav) is kept in `data/replay/runs/<id>/<runId>/`.
